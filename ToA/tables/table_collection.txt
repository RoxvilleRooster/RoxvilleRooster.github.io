### **1. Chase Events (1d20)**

```text
1|Large Crowd: A group (herd, procession, etc.) impedes progress. If not managed or bypassed (via Persuasion, Intimidation, etc.), the opposing side changes the gap by two lengths.
2|Dangerous Hazard: Physical threat (riot, poisonous vines, slime). 1d4 characters must make a Luck (Dex) save or suffer 2d6 + level damage.
3|Obstruction: Physical obstruction (horse & cart, thick scrub, winds). If not bypassed or resolved, the opposing side changes the gap by 1d2 lengths.
4|Snap Opportunity: 1d4 characters from both sides may take one action (attack, spell, etc.) to assist themselves or hinder the opposition, provided they declare it within a 5-second countdown.
5|Random Setback: Roll 1d6: (i) Drop Item (Battle Gear), (ii) Fatigue (Leader is Fatigued), (iii) Head Strike (Con save or Stunned), (iv) Fall (Dex check or trip), (v) Hostile Third Party (Random encounter), (vi) Lost (Int check or separated).
6|Burst of Speed: The party attempts a Group Con (Athletics) check. On a success, they put on a burst of speed and change the gap by one length.
7|Dangerous Hazard: Falling debris, hunting trap, or hostile bird. The leader must make a Luck (Dex) save or roll 1d12 on an appropriate Trauma table.
8|Obstruction: Barrels, crates, jammed door, or smoke. If not bypassed or resolved, the opposing side changes the gap by two lengths.
9|Random Setback: As Entry #5.
10|Hidey Hole: Quarry locate a hiding place. Group Dex (Stealth) check (if PCs are quarry) or Group Perc (Detection) check (if PCs are chasers). Success means quarry escape; failure means they are caught. Either way, chase ends.
11|Snap Opportunity: As Entry #4.
12|Third Party: A small number of creatures (beggars, rock grinders, sprites) impede progress. If not managed or bypassed, the opposing side changes the gap by 1d2 lengths.
13|Fell Vapours: Gaseous or inhalation threat (poison vapours, carbon dioxide, spores). 1d4 PCs must make a Luck (Con) save or roll 1d12 on the Toxin Trauma table.
14|Cross Paths: A random chaser catches up to a lagging quarry. Each character may make a single melee attack against the other before being separated again.
15|Split Up: Opponents split. If quarry split, party chooses which group to focus on (may require mini-chase). If chasers split, half the NPCs are fatigued and drop out.
16|Random Setback: As Entry #5.
17|Major Obstruction: Busy marketplace, mud, hail storm. If not resolved, opposition changes gap by 1d2 lengths. Random PC makes Luck (Dex) save or a pack tear causes loss of random Pack Gear.
18|Burst of Speed: The opposition is inspired to put on a burst of speed, changing the gap by two lengths! The party may attempt a group Luck save to negate this.
19|All In!: The whole party and equal number of opponents may take an action (attack, spell, etc.) provided each player declares within a 5-second countdown.
20|Hidey Hole: As Entry #10, but after the chase ends, all PCs must make a Luck (Con) save or be Fatigued.
```

### **2. Fancy Meeting You Here (1d6)**

```text
1|Lone Survivor: A lone survivor of an adventuring party, lost in the tunnels. Half hp & rations, no torches. Might know about nearby monsters.
2|Prisoner: A prisoner of the next monster or chained up to a pillar, makeshift shrine, in a pit or cell, etc. Dazed and confused, Fatigued, no gear.
3|I’m Standing Right Here: The PC was always with the party, just in the background as a torch bearer, rear sentry, porter, etc. Similar injuries and resources used as the other PCs.
4|I’ve Got a Bad Feeling About This: PC was minding the horses or guarding the entrance, but felt something was wrong and came to help. Same rations as party, but horses are lost and camp gear is stolen, trashed, or burnt when the party eventually departs.
5|Stone Curse: The PC is the victim of a decades-old Veil curse encasing them in stone. Freeing the PC by breaking the rock triggers an instant DDM effect. No rations or torches, and the character has aged an extra 1d10+20 years.
6|Belly of the Beast: A hostile ten-foot burrowing worm (as Plague Worm but AC 12) attacks! If killed, the new PC is found in its belly, alive but starving with 5 hp and no gear.
```

```text

11|A Random Encounter occurs.
12|Camaraderie: One player reveals a PC secret. If entertained, each PC recovers 1 Luck or takes a Short Rest.
13|Short Cut: Resolve as Int or Perc (Wilderness Lore) check. On a Great Success the party enters this hex for free.
14|Lost Supplies: Group Luck save or rations/water halved. Tainted food requires Luck (Con) save vs disease/poison.
15|A Random Encounter occurs.
16|Discovery: Find hex secret or a Monster Lair (use Lair numbers).

18|Taxing Terrain (chasm, collapse, etc). Montage (Hard, 2F) required. Failure: party is Fatigued and loses gear.
19|A Random Encounter occurs.
20|Perfect Campsite: Perc (Wilderness Lore*) check. Success: PCs restore 1d3 attribute points, one Reroll, or one Luck point.
```
